# Winning in Style

Big plays. Shared synergies. Flashy finishes.

Commander-style stories in a normal draft. No commanders required.

**First draft?** [Open the visual primer](https://cube.coolasheck.com/primer). [Cube health](https://cube.coolasheck.com/) shows exact support.

## Draft in four moves

**Pick one. Pass the rest.**

**Draft interaction.** Your opponent will have strong cards too. Give yourself a few ways to deal with a creature you can't beat in combat.

**Fixing gives you permission to play more colors.** Take lands and fixing seriously.

**Build 40 cards.** Start near 17 lands.

## Pick a plan

### {W}{U} Azorius / Blink

You get more out of creatures whose abilities trigger when they enter. Blink them to use those abilities again.

<<[[!Conjurer's Closet|7e520d49-f3d5-46b3-a941-c57936648a47]][[!Spirited Companion|f2d28a16-e119-456d-ad8b-c37cec069ab0]]>>

### {W}{B} Orzhov / Aristocrats

Look for creatures you don't mind losing, and cards that reward you when they die.

<<[[!Guildsworn Prowler|d7efb10f-c760-431c-8ac6-904965d850dc]][[!Blood Artist|c4c1641d-c4ea-4657-a0ae-db09bba83a0f]]>>

### {G}{W} Selesnya / Counters + go wide

Make several creatures, then improve them together with counters. A small board can turn into a real attack.

<<[[!United Front|d347caf8-0c12-401f-9e33-9978cb347f89]][[!Kalonian Hydra|9a936dcd-684a-47f6-860c-2f3541d8286c]]>>

### {B}{R} Rakdos / Artifact sacrifice

An artifact can do a job twice: once when it enters, and again when you sacrifice it for another effect.

<<[[!Ichor Wellspring|1ccdb407-ac8f-4736-89d3-ab0d086096ea]][[!Legion Extruder|5a077de0-1893-40d0-a499-ee2e6e2258f1]]>>

### {U}{G} Simic / +1/+1 counters

+1/+1 counters let your smaller creatures keep growing. Look for cards that add counters and cards that multiply them.

<<[[!Essence Capture|ce137910-0f0e-4f94-9b95-6e0eeeba164e]][[!Kalonian Hydra|9a936dcd-684a-47f6-860c-2f3541d8286c]]>>

### {R}{G} Gruul / Power 4+

Creatures with at least four power turn on cards like The Earth King. Get that board in place, then attack.

<<[[!Surrak, Elusive Hunter|e4775a26-0b66-40e9-8f64-41d9308ca032]][[!The Earth King|a8d5dca6-381a-4361-b265-27de8d04335c]]>>

### {B}{G} Golgari / Delirium

Delirium needs four card types in your graveyard. Cards that mill you can help turn it on.

<<[[!Stitcher's Supplier|2edcde06-b326-476e-884d-770187c785fe]][[!Demolisher Spawn|16b97600-e4cf-4b9e-8f2d-2eb74298eb54]]>>

### {W}{R} Boros / Humans

Humans can make each other stronger. Cheap Humans let you build the board before cards like Thalia's Lieutenant reward you.

<<[[!Cathar Commando|7cd21530-ca72-4986-a0f2-142b9f23c413]][[!Thalia's Lieutenant|3b619bda-12ff-4390-bda7-af0a44e5f955]]>>

### {U}{R} Izzet / Noncombat damage

Small damage adds up. Pair repeatable damage from cards like Firebrand Archer with effects that make each hit larger.

<<[[!Firebrand Archer|2d176b57-4793-485e-b033-7ba668634f62]][[!Solphim, Mayhem Dominus|c3a0c7f3-7fb9-43de-a9af-96532c31e5ed]]>>

### {U}{B} Dimir / Theft

You can use an opponent's spells as part of your own plan. Keep enough interaction to reach the turn when it matters.

<<[[!Extract Brain|8c3a4a44-5e75-42a4-8140-2105c2becff9]][[!Smirking Spelljacker|01d1e394-1b0c-4b40-ba85-f55973703d41]]>>

### {W}{U}{B}{R}{G} Multicolor / Five-color good stuff

Draft fixing early. Build a strong base color. Splash powerful cards when your mana supports them; five colors is an option, not a requirement.
