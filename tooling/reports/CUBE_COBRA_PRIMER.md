# Winning in Style

Big plays. Shared synergies. Flashy finishes.

Born from Commander-style stories, played as a normal draft: no commanders required. Blink is the heart of the cube, with ten two-color starting points and room to branch out.

**First draft?** [The visual five-minute guide](https://style.coolasheck.com/draft-primer.html) covers picks, removal, mana curve and your 40-card deck. [Cube health](https://style.coolasheck.com/) shows the exact support and intersections.

## Your draft in four moves

**Pick one, pass the rest.** Packs usually pass left, right, then left. Follow the host's pack setup.

**Take removal seriously.** Efficient answers stop opposing engines and clear attacks. Balance them with creatures and a way to win.

**Start with two colors.** Add a third only when your lands can cast it. Cards that support several plans keep your options open.

**Build 40 cards.** Start near 17 lands, 15 creatures and 8 other spells. Removal is part of those spells, not extra cards. Adjust to your deck.

## Keep the lanes open

A card can do more than one job. Enter-the-battlefield creatures can bridge Blink into artifacts, counters or Humans. The health site keeps additional themes accessible without confusing them with the designed pairs.

Choose a plan, draft enough early plays, pick answers, and make something worth remembering.
