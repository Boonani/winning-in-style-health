Your first cube draft

Five minutes. One plan. A deck you can actually cast.

01 / Pick and pass

You build your deck as you go

PACK 1Pick 1Pass left ←
PACK 2Pick 1Pass right →
PACK 3Pick 1Pass left ←

Keep one card, pass the rest, then choose from the pack you receive. Repeat until the packs are empty. Follow the host's pack setup.

Start with strong, flexible cards. Settle into two colors as good cards keep coming. Your first pick is not a promise.

02 / Answers matter

Take good removal highly

Removal gets a threat out of your way. It can stop their best engine or make your winning attack possible.

Prefer answers that are cheap and hit many threats. Do not spend your only answer on something harmless. You still need creatures and a way to win.

03 / Build 40 cards

A useful starting recipe

17 lands15 creatures8 other spells

Removal is inside the spell slots, not added on top. Some creatures are also removal. This is a starting point, not a quota.

Basic lands are provided; draft special lands that help cast your colors. Add a third color only with reliable mana support, not just one matching land.

04 / Play on time

More early plays. Fewer expensive cards.

One example for 15 creatures. Two- and three-mana plays help you attack or block before big cards arrive. A cheap combat trick is not a creature you can play early.

05 / Pick a plan

Blink is the heart of this cube

Payoff: rewards your plan. Enabler: makes it happen.

Blink briefly exiles a permanent, then returns it. That can reuse an enter-the-battlefield reward. Check what the blink effect can target and when it returns.

Begin with a two-color core. These are starting points, not ten isolated decks.
