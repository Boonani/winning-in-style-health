# Oscar's Cube Design Direction

Source: Oscar's pasted request and supplied George Fan Plants vs. Zombies tutorial transcript, September 5, 2026. This is a project design brief, not a global memory update.

## Nonnegotiables

- Rename the Style subdomain to Cube. The verified owned zone is coolasheck.com (the speech transcript says CoolestHeck.com).
- QR card opens the primer. Supply a 63 x 88 mm card and a printable sheet.
- Phone and iOS first. All information fits the viewport without clipped text or document-wide sideways scrolling.
- Show one Magic card per row, never competing side by side, including dashboard/history. Full available phone width, uncropped artwork.
- Prefer recent English printings with short, clear readable rules. Do not substitute textless art or alter the live cube's printing choices.
- Replace removal advice with exactly: Games are often won based on who is able to interact more.
- Remove the Blink-is-the-heart heading and payoff/enabler boilerplate from the primer. Leave owner-authored Blink copy blank rather than inventing replacement prose.
- Provide a private front-end editor for text, card ordering and printing changes. Saved authoring content must survive reloads and crashes. No unauthenticated public write endpoint.
- Clear color hierarchy and restrained, polished typography. Subtle easing for new or removed information. Mana bars rise as they appear.
- Scroll reveals should fade softly near the viewport edges, never obstruct or distract from reading. Respect reduced-motion, keyboard and no-JavaScript use.
- Keep the source in an organized Git project on this computer, preserve history, push verified delivery, and organize Cube tasks in Codex.

## George Fan Review Lens

The supplied transcript emphasizes: blend teaching into play; let the player do before reading; stagger mechanics; prompt one action once; use fewer words; avoid interrupting flow; adapt explanations to need; do not create noise; teach visually; leverage familiar ideas. The 'sophisticated caveman' rule means concise grammatical language, not deliberately broken sentences. Eight words is an aspirational prompt budget, not a reason to omit essential meaning or change Oscar's exact requested sentence.

Apply this as one idea per section, short literal headings, visuals at the moment they help, optional detail rather than mandatory walls of text, no redundant narration, and no motion competing with the card being read.

## Boundaries

This change owns the website, presentation metadata and primer editor. It does not change Cube Cobra card membership, tags, deck rules, taxonomy, or CubeArena's engine. Browser-engine checks are not physical iPhone testing.
